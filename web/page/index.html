Raytheon 703

A 1967 sixteen-bit computer, emulated. Its front panel above, its teletype below.

Operating it

  • The machine powers on halted, the HALT lens lit. Press RUN on the panel.
  • Click the paper, then type. The Model 33 has capitals only. Return is CARRIAGE RETURN, shift-Return is LINE FEED, and Backspace sends RUBOUT.
  • It types at ten characters a second, as the teletype did, and a key typed while the machine is busy can be lost, as it was. Fast I/O makes the teletype and the disc instant.
  • Under REX, HELP lists the commands, DIR 0: the programs on the disc, and RUN BASIC starts Tiny BASIC.
  • On the panel, HALT and SINGLE COMMAND stop and step the machine. The lamps are switches too: click one to key in that bit. The knob picks the register in the lower row: click it, or with the panel focused press the left and right arrows. Keys 1 to 4 flip the SENSE switches.
