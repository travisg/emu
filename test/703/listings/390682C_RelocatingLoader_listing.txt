RELOCATING LOADER - BASIC, DN 390682 (Revision C), 08/9/68, ID code BNP
Assembly listing (Appendix A of the scanned document), transcribed by hand
from the 600 dpi page images.  The PDF has no OCR text layer.

Source: 390682C_RelocatingLoaderBasic_Nov1968.pdf, 52 pages.  Pages 1-21 are
prose and flowcharts; page 23 is the "APPENDIX A / ASSEMBLY LISTING" divider;
pages 24-51 are the listing, printed as PAGE 2 through PAGE 29.  Listing PAGE 1
is not in the scan -- the divider sheet stands in its place -- so card 1 is
lost, exactly as it is for X-RAY.  Everything from card 2 on is present.

Layout of a listing line, reproduced as printed:

    ADDR    OBJ   FIELDS    CARD  LABEL   OP    OPERAND      COMMENT
    0 63D 0 7546  7 0 546   500           STW   BIAS          SAVE IT

  ADDR    word address, with the assembler's leading and trailing digits
  OBJ     the assembled word
  FIELDS  the same word split into opcode / index bit / M field.  This is
          redundant with OBJ and is the transcription's built-in check: the
          fields must recompose to the word.
  CARD    source card number, 1..1786
  ...     label, mnemonic, operand and comment as punched

Lines with no ADDR/OBJ generated no object code: they are comment cards (a
quote or an asterisk in column 1), or directives that produce no word, or the
TRUE/FALS/ENDC conditionals themselves.

CARD NUMBERS ARE NOT CONTIGUOUS, and this listing differs from X-RAY's in the
way that matters most.  When a conditional is not taken, its body is not
printed at all -- not printed without object code, simply absent, with its card
numbers missing from the sequence.  Card 5 is `TRUE LOADER=STANDARD` and card 7
is its `ENDC`; card 6 is nowhere on the page.  X-RAY's listing printed untaken
bodies in full, which is why a third of that transcript is code that never
assembled.  Here there is nothing to transcribe: the source of the STANDARD and
DISK loaders is not in this document.

So a gap in the card numbers is normal and is not a dropped line.  The
conditional and its ENDC always print, and page boundaries are contiguous.

The right margin of the printed page carries the punched-card sequence field,
"NP nnnnn", one per card.  It is not transcribed -- the card number column
already gives a running count -- and neither is the page number beside it,
which appears in the PAGE headings below.

This assembly is LOADER EQU BASIC.  Every card printed here was assembled, so
every card has printed object to check a transcription against -- which is not
true of X-RAY's listing.

Trailer of the original listing: CARDS 1786 SYMBOLS 175 583 LITR 0 STACK 6.
